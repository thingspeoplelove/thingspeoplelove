## Hello, fellow human. 👋

We're **Things People Love**, an independent creative studio based in Dallas, Texas, making games, digital products, and interactive experiences that spark wonder, build connection, and stay with you.

We exist at the intersection of art, design, technology, and play.

We're driven by curiosity, fueled by creativity, and powered by joy. We believe technology can be a little more human, games can be a little more meaningful, and everyday experiences can be a hell of a lot more fun.

We take what we make seriously.

We try not to take ourselves too seriously.

---

## Small Studio. Big Feelings.

Built on a foundation of 25+ years of creative and game development experience, Things People Love brings together creative direction, product design, software engineering, and emerging technologies under one independent studio.

We're interested in what happens when the boundaries between artist, designer, and developer start to disappear.

We build things from the ground up, from the first ridiculous idea scribbled in a notebook to the systems, code, characters, and experiences that bring it to life.

We believe being small is a creative advantage.

Fewer barriers. More experimentation. Better conversations. More room for the weird little ideas that might turn into something wonderful.

### Word Scrapes

**WORDS ARE WEAPONS.**

*Come save the kingdom. Bring a dictionary.*

Build words, battle monsters, discover heroes, explore whimsical worlds, and prove that the pen might actually be mightier than the sword.

Equal parts word game and role-playing adventure, Word Scrapes combines accessible gameplay with character progression, strategic encounters, and a world full of wonderfully peculiar inhabitants.

## How We Build

We believe the best creative work happens when art, design, and engineering inform one another rather than exist in isolation.

Our approach brings the entire product development process together.

### From Idea to Experience

1. **Explore** — Ask questions, challenge assumptions, and find the interesting problems.
2. **Imagine** — Establish the creative vision, identity, and experience.
3. **Prototype** — Make things quickly, test ideas, and embrace experimentation.
4. **Build** — Develop the frontend, backend, systems, and content that bring the experience to life.
5. **Refine** — Test, iterate, simplify, and make the little things better.
6. **Share** — Put things into the world, listen, learn, and keep improving.

Good ideas deserve good execution.

## Human Creativity. Artificial Intelligence.

We embrace AI as part of our creative and technical toolkit.

Not because creativity needs replacing, but because creative people deserve better tools.

We're interested in using emerging technologies to help independent creators and small teams build experiences that might otherwise require significantly larger production resources.

### AI-Assisted Development

We use AI-assisted engineering workflows to support:

- Application architecture and implementation
- Frontend and backend development
- Feature development and rapid prototyping
- Debugging, refactoring, and testing
- Technical documentation
- Agent-driven implementation workflows

### Generative Asset Pipelines

We're developing repeatable creative workflows that combine generative technologies with intentional human art direction.

These workflows explore:

- API-driven asset generation
- Consistent character and environment design
- Structured prompt systems
- Parameter-driven content generation
- Automated asset preparation
- Scalable visual production
- Human-led quality control

### Designing Systems, Not Just Outputs

A single interesting image or a working piece of code is only the beginning.

We're interested in the systems behind them: reusable design languages, production standards, technical frameworks, creative guardrails, and workflows that make quality repeatable.

Our approach prioritizes consistency, maintainability, intentionality, and creative ownership.

AI helps us explore more possibilities.

Human judgment determines which possibilities are worth pursuing.
